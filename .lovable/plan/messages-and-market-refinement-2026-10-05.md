# Messages and Market refinement

## Messages
- Create a spacious WhatsApp-inspired inbox and conversation layout with larger photos, readable names, clear controls, and a subtle fading emerald watermark over light and dark surfaces.
- Replace the small three-dot popup with an animated, focused options panel over a blurred background. Keep pin, archive, lock, and selection behavior; make pinned status clear without covering names or timestamps.
- Make member photos open a blurred, animated choice panel with available Story and Spotlight actions, plus the existing profile action. Reuse existing safety-filtered content and viewing experiences; show only available content.
- Preserve messages, attachments, groups, support, AI, unread counts, and chat security.

## Market and Extras
- Make Market the default main tab, including its name and marketplace icon, even for users who previously selected Services.
- Keep the current Market listings, Deals and Services categories, search, filters, and selling actions.
- Add Extras immediately after Services. Open a dedicated screen with the existing shortcut destinations arranged in a named app-style row-and-column grid.
- Give ASH-owned apps cohesive ASH-branded icon artwork; retain third-party identities and existing opening behavior.

## Technical approach
- Keep the existing Services URL and commerce queries; use a separate Extras leaf for a shareable screen and reuse the current shortcut destinations.
- Keep visual colors scoped to Messages and defined as semantic tokens; use accessible dialog controls with reduced-motion support.
- Verify both themes, phone and desktop layouts, options/avatars, Market default, Extras navigation, and existing tests without publishing.
